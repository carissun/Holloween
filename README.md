## Holloween

A little Halloween themed game made in p5.js (my first try with p5.js :D)

### About

You've missed Halloween, the bat skull (yup, I drew it) gives you a chance to recover from the damages. 

There are four main little levels, in which you click on bats and the bats follow a path that reveals a clue. Then, you solve the final level using those clues.

### Features
- I used this [guide](https://ghostship.hackclub.com/tutorial/) to get started with p5.js   
- The art is made by me and I've added little sounds (using Tone.js).
- A little transition of the last bat you clicked going into a hiding and onto the next level.
- The little effect of the background gradually turning black at the start screen and then transitions into the title of the game.
- I used this ['Splotchy Chalk Text'](https://codepen.io/fractalkitty/pen/EaVaoJO) example from FreeFrontend.com's p5.js examples, as the reference for the title of the game.
